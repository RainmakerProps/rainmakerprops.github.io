How it works

A model, not a hunchProjects minutes, points, rebounds and assists for every player, then compares them with DraftKings' lines.

Only medium edgesPicks are spots where the model disagrees with the line by a modest amount. Its biggest disagreements have proven to be its worst.

Flat 1 unitEvery pick is the same size, so the record can't be dressed up by bet sizing.

Graded automaticallyResults come straight from the NBA's box scores the next morning.
